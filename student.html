Help & Support Center

Frequently Asked Questions

What if my internet disconnects?

The A-UOS engine includes an offline mode. Do not refresh or close the tab. Your answers will continue to save locally to your device. A red warning banner will appear at the top of the screen. Once your connection is restored, the exam will sync automatically.

Why do I get security alerts?

The portal utilizes strict lockdown rules. If you attempt to switch browser tabs, exit fullscreen mode, or use keyboard shortcuts, a violation is recorded and sent to the administrator. The admin has the power to manually terminate your exam session if repeated violations are flagged.

How does the drawing canvas work?

The canvas accepts mouse clicks, touch input, and stylus/pen pressure. You can adjust line thickness and switch to an eraser tool in the top toolbar. Depending on the exam rules, you may also have access to highlighters, shapes, colors, and unlimited scrolling pages.
